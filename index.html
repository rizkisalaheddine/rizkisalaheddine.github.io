Salah-Eddine Rizki

PhD Candidate in Artificial Intelligence at ETIS Laboratory, ENSEA & CY Cergy Paris University

Paris region, France

I am a PhD candidate in artificial intelligence at ETIS Laboratory, ENSEA, and CY Cergy Paris University, advised by Vassilis Christophides. My research focuses on graph representation learning and geometric deep learning, with current applications in molecular modeling and machine-learning interatomic potentials. I am also interested in generative modeling and explainable AI.
