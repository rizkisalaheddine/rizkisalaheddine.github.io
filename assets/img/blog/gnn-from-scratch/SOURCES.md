## Oversmoothing plots

`oversmoothing-cosine.svg` and `oversmoothing-dispersion.svg` were generated with Matplotlib from the three-seed benchmark results reported in the companion repository. The solid curves are seed means, and the shaded regions represent one sample standard deviation.
